# Pitch Summary

## Problem

Traditional casino platforms often use a house advantage model where the platform mathematically benefits from player losses.

## Solution

OBBG creates a peer-to-peer model where users compete against each other in cultural board games. The platform is the match host, not the player's opponent.

## Business model

The product concept uses a transparent match fee from the demo pot. In a future real-money version, the exact model must be reviewed legally and launched only with licensing or a licensed partner.

## Differentiation

- No house opponent
- Cultural game focus
- Skill-first positioning
- Transparent fee model
- Replay and fairness system
- Demo wallet only in prototype
- Legal-first roadmap

## Current status

Frontend prototype with fake user, demo wallet, game lobby, match setup, simplified Oware-inspired match room, demo payout logic, leaderboard, profile and legal pages.
