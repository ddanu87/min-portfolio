# OBBG Concept

OBBG stands for Online Betting Board Games.

The idea is to create a peer-to-peer platform where players compete against each other in cultural board games instead of playing against a casino house.

The platform does not play against users. It only facilitates matches, locks the demo stake, verifies the winner and takes a transparent service fee.

This prototype is demo-only and does not support real-money gaming.

## Product position

OBBG should be presented as a cultural competitive gaming platform first and a regulated real-money product only after legal approval.

## First playable focus

The first playable game should be a low-chance, skill-first board game such as an Oware/Mancala-inspired game. Higher-chance games such as dice modes should stay as concept-only until the legal path is clear.
