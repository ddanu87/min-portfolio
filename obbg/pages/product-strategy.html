Core positioning

Play people, not the house.

OBBG is a peer-to-peer cultural board game platform where players compete against each other instead of playing against a casino house.

Danish tagline: Spil mod mennesker, ikke mod huset.

Core Features

The product should be structured around six core systems.

1. Game Lobby

Choose game, stake level, ranked/unranked, casual/tournament, rules, chance level, skill level and estimated payout.

2. Wallet

Balance, locked pot, transaction history, fee history, limits, withdrawal status and future regulated payments.

3. Matchmaking

Elo/ranking, same skill-level, ranked queues, anti-smurfing, cooldowns and suspicious behavior detection.

4. Game Engine

Deterministic rules, server-side validation, move history, no client-side result manipulation and replay logs.

5. Fairness

No house opponent, transparent rules, visible fee, replay after match, dispute system and clear payout calculation.

6. Safety

Age checks, KYC, AML, exclusion tools, deposit limits, cooling-off periods and responsible play warnings.

First MVP

Do not start with 20 games. Start with one strong playable game and one concept mode.

First playable game

Digital Oware / Mancala

Best first MVP game because it is culturally strong, strategic, visually clear, low-randomness, skill-based and good for ranking.

Concept mode only first

Dice Culture Mode

Strong for engagement, but high chance. Keep it demo-only until legal review, licensing and safety controls are ready.

MVP Roadmap

MVP 1

Oware/Mancala-inspired game, fake wallet, ranking, matchmaking flow, replay-log concept and demo payout.

MVP 2

Tournaments, leaderboard, user profiles, match history and anti-cheat logic.

MVP 3

Dice mode as demo only. No real-money dice mode before legal review.

MVP 4

Legal review, license path, KYC, AML and responsible play setup.

MVP 5

Regulated launch only through license or licensed partner.
