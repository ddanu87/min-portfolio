Legal-first roadmap

From prototype to licensed launch

OBBG cannot offer real-money games without the correct licence, compliance and approval. This roadmap keeps the project serious.

1. Prototype

Demo only. No real money. No deposits. No withdrawals.

2. Playable MVP

One game, fake wallet, rankings, match history and clear product flow.

3. Closed beta

User testing without real-money features.

4. Legal review

Gambling lawyer, classification, licensing path and compliance scope.

5. Licensed partner or restricted licence

KYC, AML, responsible gaming, payment compliance and operational controls.

6. Real-money launch

Only after legal approval and correct licensing setup.

OBBG cannot offer real-money games without the correct licence and compliance setup.
