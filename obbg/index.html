Problem

Traditional casino platforms often use a house advantage model where the platform mathematically benefits from player losses.

House advantage

The platform is often the opponent or controls the odds.

Low transparency

Players do not always understand fees, odds or expected losses.

Weak culture layer

Many games ignore the cultural board game traditions people already know.

Solution

OBBG positions the platform as a host, not as the house. Players compete against each other in cultural board games and the service fee is transparent.
